# FAQ and contact

Last updated: 2026-11-26 · v0.1.0

**Why does mail land in spam?**
The domain needs SPF, DKIM, and DMARC, and new tenants send slowly at first. See [deliverability](./deliverability.md).

**Can I keep my old mail?**
Yes. Import from the previous host is on the roadmap. See [migration](./migration.md).

**Where is data stored?**
India, by default. Other regions are an Enterprise option later.

**Which plan do I need?**
Free, Starter, Business, Business Plus, or Enterprise. Numbers are on [limits](./limits.md).

Support channels per plan, plus `abuse@` and `postmaster@`, will be published when mailboxes exist. Until then, product questions stay in the repository.
